## Brand & Style
This design system sets a definitive, premier standard for next-generation career preparation. Targeted at high-caliber software engineers, senior product leaders, and ambitious executives, the aesthetic projects supreme technological confidence, focus, and quiet luxury. The visual atmosphere evokes the feeling of commanding an advanced intelligence cockpit: deliberate, distraction-free, and razor-sharp.

The design identity fuses **Futuristic Glassmorphism** with **Minimalist Precision**. Deep void surfaces (`#030712`) are illuminated by atmospheric, multi-point ambient mesh glows in electric violet, cybernetic cyan, and cobalt. Surface structures feel lightweight yet structural, utilizing frosted glass cards with hairline specular borders, subtle backdrop filters, and crisp interactive luminescence to create a refined hierarchy.

## Colors
The color palette is built on deep cosmic darks punctuated by luminous, neon-driven energy streams:

- **Primary (`#6366F1` - Electric Indigo):** Anchors critical actions, AI active listening states, and focal points.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` - Neon Cyan):** Signals active telemetry, real-time speech processing, and audio frequency meters.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6` - Vivid Violet):** Used for ambient gradient meshes, executive coaching feedback badges, and high-tier insights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#030712` to `#0B0F19`):** Pure deep slate and obsidian tones that absorb visual noise and optimize contrast for prolonged interview sessions.

### Functional Roles & Gradients
- **Active Gradients:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #6366F1 0%, #8B5CF6 50%, #06B6D4 100%)` for primary gradient borders and hero CTAs.
- **Success & Status:** Emerald `#10B981` (optimal interview metrics/clarity scores), Amber `#F59E0B` (pacing warning), Rose `#F43F5E` (filler word/critical alert).
- **Surface Elevation Layers:** Surface base (`#030712`), Surface Container Low (`#0B0F19` at 80% opacity), Surface Container Med (`#111827` at 65% opacity), Surface Container High (`#1F2937` at 50% opacity).

## Typography
The typographic system establishes rigorous hierarchy and effortless legibility under demanding real-time conditions:

- **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern, geometric, and crisp. Headlines employ tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to project intentionality and punchy executive weight.
- **Body & Content (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and meticulously kerned. Ideal for candidate responses, structured feedback dossiers, and resume parsing side-by-sides.
- **Technical & Metrics (JetBrains Mono):** Dedicated to speech metrics, audio latency, sentiment scoring, timecodes, and system diagnostics.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column grid system bounded at a maximum container width of `1440px`.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 48px (`3rem`) margins. Multi-pane arrangements (e.g., live candidate video feed, AI interviewer avatar pane, and real-time transcript stream) align to 4-col and 8-col modular splits.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Panels collapse into stacked split views with quick-switch tab navigation.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with 12px gutters and 16px margins. Telemetry and live transcripts prioritize bottom-sheet overlays to preserve candidate framing.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through optical transparency, chromatic refraction, and targeted backlighting rather than muddy drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Void):** Pure dark background (`#030712`) enriched with fixed SVG radial glow meshes (`opacity: 0.15`, `filter: blur(120px)` in cyan, cobalt, and violet).
- **Level 1 (Glass Surface):** Background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)`, backdrop filter `blur(16px)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active Card):** Background `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.65)`, backdrop filter `blur(24px)`, border `1px solid rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.3)`, subtle glow shadow `0 0 24px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Command Center):** Background `rgba(10, 15, 29, 0.85)`, backdrop filter `blur(32px)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`, shadow `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 32px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`.

## Shapes
The design uses roundedness level 2 (0.5rem base radius). Cards, preview screens, and panels use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to feel welcoming and sleek. Buttons, badges, and pill chips use generous squircle radii (`9999px`) or `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to balance futuristic geometry with organic ergonomics.

## Components

### 1. High-Contrast CTA Buttons
- **Primary Gradient CTA:** Background uses a rich gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #6366F1, #8B5CF6)` enclosed in an outer border pseudo-element with subtle cyan glow. On hover, the internal gradient shifts and expands a soft drop glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Glass Button:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)` border, transitioning to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` on hover with crisp white text.

### 2. Frosted Glass Cards
Feature subtle directional edge illumination: a top-border highlight (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25), transparent)`) simulating overhead cockpit light refraction over a dark backdrop blur.

### 3. Glowing Badges & AI Status Indicators
- Badges use pill shapes with monospace labels (`JetBrains Mono`).
- AI state badges (e.g., "AI LISTENING", "ANALYZING SPEECH CADENCE") feature a pulsating circular neon indicator (`#06B6D4` or `#6366F1`) paired with a micro-glow backdrop (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)`).

### 4. Input Fields & Prompt Consoles
- Dark obsidian base (`rgba(3, 7, 18, 0.6)`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Focused state triggers an electric cyan perimeter glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #06B6D4, 0 0 16px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`).

### 5. AI Audio & Video Specialized Components
- **Voice Waveform Visualizer:** 32 vertical equalizer bars utilizing multi-stop cyan-to-violet linear gradients that dynamically scale in amplitude with candidate and interviewer voice frequencies.
- **Feedback Telemetry Cards:** Micro-charts with glowing stroke graphs displaying real-time speech velocity (words per minute), confidence score percentages, and conciseness indicators.
